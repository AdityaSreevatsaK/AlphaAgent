# Initial Company Selection Rationale

**Date**: 20th July 2025.

### Context

For the initial analysis phase of the **AlphaAgent** project, a representative and diverse set of publicly traded companies was required. The primary goal was to select companies that would provide a rich and varied dataset for testing the agent's data ingestion, analysis, and language processing capabilities across different industries and markets.

### Decision Rationale

The selection strategy was to create a balanced portfolio covering key global sectors and market leaders. This includes representation from both the US and other major international markets (India, South Korea) to ensure the agent is robust enough to handle various financial reporting styles, market conditions, and industry-specific terminology.

The selected companies are:

* **Technology (Mega-Cap US)**:
    * `AAPL` (Apple Inc.): A benchmark for consumer electronics and brand loyalty. Its financials are closely watched globally, providing a strong baseline.
    * `MSFT` (Microsoft Corporation): Represents a complex blend of enterprise software, cloud computing (Azure), and consumer hardware, offering diverse language and business segments.
    * `NVDA` (NVIDIA Corporation): A key player in the AI and semiconductor space. Essential for understanding the technology sector's cutting edge and supply chain.

* **E-commerce & Cloud**:
    * `AMZN` (Amazon.com, Inc.): A leader in both e-commerce and cloud services (AWS), presenting a unique and complex business model for the agent to deconstruct.

* **Finance**:
    * `JPM` (JPMorgan Chase & Co.): One of the largest US banks, providing insight into the health of the financial sector and its specific regulatory language.

* **Automotive & Advanced Manufacturing**:
    * `TSLA` (Tesla, Inc.): A high-growth, high-volatility stock that is as much a technology and energy company as it is a car manufacturer.
    * `TM` (Toyota Motor Corporation): Represents a traditional, established global automotive leader, providing a valuable point of comparison against a disruptor like Tesla.

* **Conglomerate/Investment**:
    * `BRK-B` (Berkshire Hathaway Inc.): Offers a unique look into a holding company with a vast and diverse portfolio, testing the agent's ability to analyse a company composed of many other businesses.

* **International Representation**:
    * `005930.KS` (Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd.): A major non-US technology and manufacturing giant from South Korea, ensuring the agent can parse and analyse international filings and market contexts.
    * `TCS.NS` (Tata Consultancy Services Limited): A leading IT services and consulting firm from India, providing exposure to the Indian market and the global IT services sector.
